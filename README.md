# privacy-statement
Peachful Privacy Policy 
Last Updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information for the mobile application “Peachful” for iOS.

We believe that your personal journal entries belong strictly to you. “Peachful” does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
- No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect any personal information, such as your name, email address, IP address, device identifier, or location data.
- Journal Content & Inputs: All journal entries and prompts within the App remain stored exclusively on your local device using built-in iOS local storage mechanisms.
No Network Transmission: Your journal entries/personal inputs are never sent to external servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

### 2. Third-Party Services & Analytics
- No Third-Party Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics frameworks, tracking pixels, or performance monitoring SDKs.
- No Advertising: "Peachful" contains no advertisements of any kind.
- No Data Sharing: Because we do not collect any data, we do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user information with third parties for any purpose.

### 3. Data Storage, Security, and Control
- On-Device Encryption: Because all data remains on your physical iOS device, your information is secured by its built-in operating system security features, including Face ID, Touch ID, device passcodes, and hardware-level storage encryption.
- Data Deletion: You maintain full control over your data. Deleting an entry within "Peachful" permanently removes it from your device storage. Uninstalling “Peachful” will completely and permanently erase all locally stored data created within it.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
- “Peachful” does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data collection occurs, "Peachful" is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy frameworks.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
- We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Any updates will be posted to this URL with an updated "Last Updated" date.

### 6. Contact Us
- If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of “Peachful”, please contact us at our email: peachfulsupport@gmail.com
